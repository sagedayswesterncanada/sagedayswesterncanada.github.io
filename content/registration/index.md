# Funding and cost

We hope to fund accommodations and meals for as many participants as possible.
At this time, we have confirmed funding to cover a limited number of participants.
Priority will be given to students and postdocs.
We have a pending funding application that would increase the number of funded participants.
For unfunded participants, the cost of accommodations and meals will be at most $544.57 CAD.
Participants are expected to pay for their own travel.

# How to apply

To participate, please [submit an application](https://forms.office.com/r/LnHqwbBMph).

For students only, we ask for a letter of support (300 words maximum) sent by
your supervisor to
[sagedays.westerncan@ucalgary.ca](mailto:sagedays.westerncan@ucalgary.ca)
(details in the application).

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Monday February 2nd | Application open |
| Friday March 13th (23:59 AOE) | Deadline to submit application |
| Friday March 27th | Notice of acceptation or rejection |
| Monday April 13th | Accepted participants confirm their intent to come or tell us that they will not come |
